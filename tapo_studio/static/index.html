Tapo account

The strips only accept local commands from someone who knows your Tapo login. It's stored encrypted on this PC and only ever sent to your strips, never anywhere else.

Can't connect?
  • The email and password are case-sensitive.
  • Your PC must be on the same Wi-Fi/network as the strip.
  • If you see "Login refused" or "FORBIDDEN": in the Tapo app go to Me → Third-Party Services → Third-Party Compatibility, switch it to the opposite setting, wait a minute, then press Reconnect.
  • Don't keep retrying a wrong password, because the strip can lock you out for a while.

Troubleshooting

Painting does nothing? Press Fix painting. Newer strip firmware needs a different request than older firmware, and the strip has to be asked which one it accepts. The app tries each in turn, shows a test pattern, and you say whether you see the left half red and the right half blue.

Run diagnostics checks each light and writes a report with no passwords, hardware IDs or full IP addresses, so it is safe to paste into a bug report.

Preferences

Privacy & security

  • Only this PC can open the control panel (it listens on 127.0.0.1).
  • Every request needs a secret key that changes each time the app starts.
  • Requests from other websites are blocked.
  • Your password is encrypted with your Windows account.
  • No accounts, cloud, tracking, cookies or outside scripts.
  • Only talks to devices on your home network.
